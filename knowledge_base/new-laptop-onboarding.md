# New Laptop Onboarding

This guide covers what new employees should do with their Acme laptop on day one, and how laptop replacements work.

## Before your first day

Your manager requests your laptop at least **5 business days** before your start date. You can choose between a Windows laptop and a MacBook; engineers may request a higher-spec model with manager approval.

## First sign-in

1. Connect to the office Wi-Fi network **Acme-Corp** or a home network.
2. Sign in with the username and temporary password from your welcome email.
3. You will be asked to choose a new password (see the Password Reset article for the policy).
4. Set up Acme Authenticator for MFA when prompted.

## First-day checklist

- Let device setup finish. Security tools and standard apps install automatically in about 30 minutes.
- Turn on disk encryption if prompted (it is mandatory).
- Sign in to email and chat.
- Request VPN access if you will work remotely.
- Request any team-specific software through the Software Request process.

## Laptop replacement and refresh

Laptops are replaced every **4 years**. You receive an email when your laptop is eligible. Broken or damaged laptops can be replaced earlier: open a ticket with a description of the damage. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported within 24 hours as a SEV2 security ticket.

## Returning a laptop

When leaving Acme or receiving a replacement, return the old laptop to the IT desk within 10 business days. Do not wipe it yourself; IT handles data removal.
