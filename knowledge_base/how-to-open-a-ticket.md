# How to Open a Ticket

When self-service articles do not solve your problem, open a ticket with the Acme Service Desk.

## Ways to open a ticket

- **Support Bot in chat**: ask the bot to open a ticket. It drafts the ticket and asks you to confirm before creating it.
- **Acme Service Portal**: `https://help.acme.example` > *Report a Problem*.
- **Phone**: Service Desk extension **4357** (+1 555 010 4357 from outside). Use the phone for SEV1 and SEV2 issues and when you cannot sign in at all.

## What to include

A good ticket has:

- A short title that names the system and the problem (for example "VPN error SC-1020 on Windows laptop").
- What you were trying to do, what happened, and the exact error message.
- When it started and whether it affects only you or others too.
- Screenshots if useful. Never include passwords or MFA codes.

## Priority

Choose a priority based on the Incident Severity Definitions: **critical** (SEV1), **high** (SEV2), **medium** (SEV3) or **low** (SEV4). If unsure, choose medium.

## After opening a ticket

You receive an email with the ticket number (format `INC-` followed by digits). Reply to that email to add information. You can follow the ticket status in the Acme Service Portal under *My Tickets*.

Tickets waiting for your reply are closed automatically after **5 business days** without a response. You can reopen a closed ticket within 10 days.
