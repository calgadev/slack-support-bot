# Printer Issues

Acme offices use **Acme Print**, a follow-me printing service. You send documents to a single queue and release them at any office printer with your badge.

## Adding the Acme Print queue

- **Windows**: the queue `AcmePrint` is installed automatically. If missing, install **Acme Print Client** from Acme Software Center.
- **macOS**: install **Acme Print Client** from Acme Self Service.

Printing works only from the office network or VPN.

## Releasing a print job

1. Tap your badge on the printer's card reader.
2. Select the jobs to print and press **Print**.

Jobs that are not released are deleted after **24 hours**. Color printing and printing more than 200 pages at once require approval from your manager in the portal.

## Badge not recognized

The first time you use a printer, it asks you to sign in with your username and password to link your badge. If the badge is still not recognized, open a ticket with your badge number.

## Paper jams and hardware problems

- Follow the on-screen instructions on the printer to clear paper jams.
- Do not open the printer's back panel or replace toner yourself unless the printer shows instructions for it.
- For hardware faults, note the **printer ID** on the label (for example `PRN-SP-0412`) and open a ticket. Use another printer in the meantime; your job stays in the queue.

## Printing from personal devices

Personal phones and laptops cannot print to Acme printers.
