# Password Reset

This article explains the Acme password policy and how to reset or unlock your account.

## Password policy

- Minimum length: **14 characters**.
- Must not contain your username or first or last name.
- Passwords expire every **180 days**. You get email reminders 14, 7 and 1 day before expiry.
- You cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords.
- Passphrases (several random words) are encouraged.

## Self-service password reset

You can reset your own password at `https://password.acme.example`.

1. Enter your Acme username.
2. Verify your identity with a push notification in Acme Authenticator or a code sent to your registered mobile number.
3. Choose a new password that follows the policy.

The new password works for your laptop, email, VPN and AcmeERP within 15 minutes.

## Account locked out

Your account locks for **30 minutes** after 5 failed sign-in attempts. You can wait, or unlock it immediately at `https://password.acme.example` with the **Unlock account** option.

A common cause of repeated lockouts is an old password saved on a phone (for example in the mail app). Update or remove the saved password on every device after a reset.

## Resetting when you cannot use self-service

If you have lost access to your MFA device and your registered phone number, call the Service Desk at extension **4357** (or +1 555 010 4357 from outside). The agent will verify your identity with your employee ID and your manager before resetting the password. The Service Desk never asks for your current password.
